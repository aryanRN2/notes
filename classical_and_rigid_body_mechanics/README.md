# ⚙️ Classical & Rigid Body Mechanics

Comprehensive master lecture notes, proofs manual, and solved problem sets for Classical and Rigid Body Mechanics (Statics & Dynamics / MTB-202).

## 📄 Contents & Master Documents
- **`complete_mechanics_proofs_manual.pdf`** / **`.tex`**: Master manual covering virtual work, central orbits, 2D general motion, SHM, constrained motion, rotating frames, and 3D forces.
- **`Tensor_Analysis_Complete_Notes.pdf`** / **`.tex`**: Complete tensor analysis definitions, coordinate transformations, Christoffel symbols, and covariant derivatives.
- **`MTB202_Statics_and_Dynamics_Notes.pdf`** / **`.tex`**: University course syllabus derivations and examination solutions.
- **`Classical_Mechanics_Statics_Notes.pdf`** / **`.tex`**: Complete statics notes, equilibrium under coplanar forces, and catenary equations.
