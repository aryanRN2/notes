# 📝 Additional Course Notes & Topics (LaTeX)

Specialized lecture notes, topic derivations, and monograph supplements typeset in LaTeX.

## 📂 Topic Collections
- **`Conformal_Mapping/`**: Complex analysis notes on Möbius transformations, conformal invariance, and mapping standard geometric domains (`conformal_mapping.pdf` / `.tex`).
- **`Quantum_Planck_Derivation/`**: Blackbody radiation, Planck's radiation law derivation, and modern physics foundations (`Planck-Derivationn.pdf`, `lat.pdf`, `lat.tex`).
- **`Machine_Learning_Foundations/`**: Foundations of ML, linear models, loss functions, and optimization (`MLF_Week1_Session1_Complete_Notes.pdf`).
- **`Geography_Master_Notes/`**: Comprehensive notes on Human Geography and Resource Planning (`GGRMN21` & `GGRMN41` PDFs and `.tex`).
- **`Group_Actions_and_Rings_NRE/`**: In-depth treatment of group actions, orbits, stabilizers, conjugacy classes, Class Equation, and matrix rings (`main.pdf` and 11 modular section `.tex` files).
- **`Calculus_and_Antiderivatives/`**: Comprehensive lecture course on differential calculus, antiderivative techniques, and analysis (`notes_complete.pdf`, `antiderivative.pdf`, and modular chapter files).
- **`Numerical_Methods_Supplements/`**: Iterative methods for nonlinear systems and complete numerical computing notes (`numerical_analysis_iterative_methods.pdf` & `Numerical_Analysis_Complete_Notes.pdf`).
- **`Academic_and_Project_Reports/`**: Academic publication templates, front matter, declarations, and internship reports in LaTeX.
