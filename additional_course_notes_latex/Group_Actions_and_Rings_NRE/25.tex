This action is also reffered as action on A by
left or left action of G on A
We can also define action of G on A from
right by considering mapping
$*: A \times G \longrightarrow A$
$(a * g_1) * g_2 = a * (g_1 g_2)$
$a * e = a \quad \forall g_1, g_2 \in G, \forall a \in A$

- Example:

1) $G, A(\neq \phi)$ s.t.
Define $*: G \times A \longrightarrow A$ as
$*(g, a) = g * a = a \quad \forall g \in G, \forall a \in A$
Let $g_1, g_2 \in G \ \& \ a \in A$
then $g_1 * (g_2 * a) = g_1 * a$
$g_1 * (g_2 * a) = a$
$\Rightarrow$ and $(g_1 g_2) * a = a$
$\Rightarrow g_1 * (g_2 * a) = (g_1 g_2) * a$
$\&$ $e * a = a$
This shows that $*$ is a group action of G on A.
This action is known as trivial G-action.

2) Take $A = G$
Define $*: G \times A \longrightarrow A$ as
$g * a = ga \in A \quad \forall g \in G, \forall a \in A = G$
$g_1 * (g_2 * a) = g_1 * (g_2 * a)$
$= g_1 * g_2 a$
$= g_1 g_2 a$
