3) $*: G \times A \to A$
$g * a = ag$
$g_1 * (g_2 * a) = g_1 * (ag_2)$
$= ag_2 g_1 \neq (g_1 g_2 * a)$
So, $*$ is not a group action.
But
If we define $*: G \times A \to A$ as
$g * a = ag^{-1}$
then $\forall e \in G$, $g_1, g_2 \in G$ s.t. $a \in A(=G)$
$g_1 * (g_2 * a) = g_1 * (ag_2^{-1})$
$= ag_2^{-1} g_1^{-1}$
$= a(g_1 g_2)^{-1}$
$= (g_1 g_2) * a$
$e * a = ae^{-1} = a$
$\therefore *$ is a group action and reffered as action of $G$ by right translation or right multiplication.
This action is also known as regular action of $G$ on itself.
