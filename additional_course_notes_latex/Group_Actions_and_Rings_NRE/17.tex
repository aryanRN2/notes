$F: G \to \text{Sym}(A)$ s.t.
$F(g) = \sigma_g$
where $\sigma_g(a) = g * a = g_a$

Let $F(g_1) = F(g_2)$
$\Rightarrow \sigma_{g_1} = \sigma_{g_2}$
$\Rightarrow \sigma_{g_1}(a) = \sigma_{g_2}(a) \quad \forall a \in A$
$\Rightarrow g_1 * a = g_2 * a \quad \forall a \in A$
$\Rightarrow g_1 * e = g_2 * a \quad \forall a \in A$
$\Rightarrow g_1 e = g_2 e$
$\Rightarrow g_1 = g_2$
$\therefore F$ is one-one
$\Rightarrow Ker F = \{e\}$
Also, by fundamental theorem of group homomorphism
$\frac{G}{Ker F} \cong F(G)$
$\frac{G}{\{e\}} \cong F(G)$
$\Rightarrow G \cong F(G)$
So, $G \cong F(G) \le \text{Sym}(A) = \text{Sym} G$

# Kernel of an action

let $*: G \times A \to A$ be a group action, then Kernel of $*$ is defined as
$Ker * = \{g \in G \mid g * a = a, \forall a \in A\}$
i.e. those elements of $G$ that fix all element of $A$.
