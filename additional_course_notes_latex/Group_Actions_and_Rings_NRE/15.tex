$Ker F = Ker *$

- Orbit and Stabilzers

Def: let $*$ be a group action of a group $G$ on a set $A$ and let $a \in A$. Then the set
$G_a = \{g \in G \mid g * a = a\}$ is called stabilizer of $a$ in $G$.

- Theorem: $G_a \le G$

Proof: $\because e \in Ker*$ For any $a \in A$
$\because e * a = a$
$\because e \in G_a \Rightarrow G_a \neq \phi$
Let $g_1, g_2 \in G_a$ for any $a \in A$
$\Rightarrow g_1 * a = a$ and $g_2 * a = a$
$\Rightarrow$ now, $(g_1 g_2) * a = g_1 * (g_2 * a) \quad (\text{as } * \text{ is group action})$
$\Rightarrow (g_1 g_2) * a = g_1 * a$
$\Rightarrow g_1 g_2 * a = a \longrightarrow (1)$
$\therefore g_1 g_2 \in G_a \longrightarrow (2)$
Also, $g_1 * a = a$
$\Rightarrow g_1^{-1} * g_1 * a = g_1^{-1} * a$
$\Rightarrow (g_1^{-1} g_1) * a = g_1^{-1} * a$
$\Rightarrow e * a = g_1^{-1} * a$
$\Rightarrow g_1^{-1} * a = a \longrightarrow (2)$
From (1) & (2) $\therefore G_a \le G$
