H.W
- Let $G$ be a group and $a, b \in G$. Then $a$ and $b$ are conjugate if $\exists c \in G$ such that $b = cac^{-1}$. Prove that $\sim$ is an equivalence relation on $G$ where $a \sim b$ when $a$ is conjugate to $b$.

Soln
let $a \in G$ then $\{\text{as } e \in G \text{ (group)}\}$

$\therefore a \sim a \text{ and } \sim \text{ is reflexive.}$
