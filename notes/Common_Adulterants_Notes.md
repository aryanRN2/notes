## 1. Overview of Food Adulteration

* **Definition:** Fraudulent addition of inferior/toxic substances or removal of vital nutrients from food to increase profit or hide poor quality.
* **Two Main Types:**
  1. **Intentional (Economic):** Deliberate adulteration for profit (e.g., watering milk, urea in synthetic milk, Vanaspati in ghee, toxic dyes in spices).
  2. **Incidental (Accidental):** Unintentional contamination from environment or processing (e.g., ergot fungi on grains, pesticide residues).

## 2. Adulteration in Staple Foods (Grains, Pulses, Flours)

### 2.1 Grains (Wheat, Rice, Bajra)
* **Talc / Soapstone ($\mathrm{Mg_3Si_4O_{10}(OH)_2}$):** Polished on rice/pulses for white shine. Irritates stomach mucosa; may carry carcinogenic tremolite asbestos. *Test:* Insoluble in boiling concentrated $\mathrm{HCl}$; high acid-insoluble ash ($>0.5\%$).
* **Mineral Oil Glaze:** Sprayed on stored grain to prevent moisture loss and insects. Impairs absorption of fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K). *Test:* Bluish-violet fluorescence under UV ($365\text{ nm}$).
* **Ergot Sclerotia (*Claviceps purpurea*):** Purplish-black fungal bodies on rye, wheat, bajra containing **Ergotamine** and **Ergocristine**.
  * *Toxicity:* Strong vasoconstriction $\rightarrow$ **Ergotism (St. Anthony's Fire)**: dry gangrene of digits, burning pain, uterine contractions (abortions), hallucinations.
  * *Test:* **$20\%\ \mathrm{NaCl}$ floatation test** — ergot floats on surface; healthy grains sink.
* **Datura Seeds:** Pitted black seeds with tropane alkaloids (**Hyoscyamine, Scopolamine**). Anticholinergic toxidrome (dilated pupils, tachycardia, dry mouth, delirium).

### 2.2 Pulses (Dhal / Legumes)
* **Khesari Dhal (*Lathyrus sativus*) in Arhar/Chana:**
  * *Appearance:* Wedge-shaped (trapezoidal, slant-cut) seeds vs. rounded Arhar dhal.
  * *Toxin:* $\beta\text{-}N\text{-oxalyl-}\alpha,\beta\text{-diaminopropionic acid}$ (**$\beta$-ODAP / BOAA**).
  * *Mechanism:* Agonist at excitatory **AMPA receptors** $\rightarrow$ excess $\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}$ influx $\rightarrow$ motor neuron lysis $\rightarrow$ **Neurolathyrism** (permanent spastic paraplegia of legs, scissoring gait).
  * *Detoxification:* $\beta$-ODAP is water-soluble; boil dhal in water ($1:10$) for 60 min and discard soak water (removes $>85\%$).
* **Metanil Yellow (Azo Dye):** Added to color inferior pulses and turmeric. Cleaved in liver to toxic **$p$-aminodiphenylamine** (testicular damage).
  * *Test:* Sample extract + **concentrated $\mathrm{HCl} \rightarrow$ persistent magenta / pink-red color** (due to quinoid hydrazone resonance). Natural turmeric fades.
* **Lead Chromate ($\mathrm{PbCrO_4}$):** Yellow industrial pigment on turmeric and pulses.
  * *Toxicity:* $\mathrm{Pb}^{2+}$ inhibits $\delta$-ALAD in heme synthesis $\rightarrow$ microcytic anemia, saturnism, brain damage.
  * *Test:* Ash sample + dilute $\mathrm{HNO_3} + \mathrm{KI} \rightarrow$ **golden-yellow precipitate of Lead Iodide ($\mathrm{PbI_2}\downarrow$)**.

### 2.3 Flours (Atta, Maida)
* **Potassium Bromate ($\mathrm{KBrO_3}$):** Flour improver that oxidizes gluten thiol ($-\mathrm{SH}$) to disulfide crosslinks ($-\mathrm{S-S-}$), inflating dough volume. IARC 2B carcinogen; strictly banned by FSSAI.
  * *Test:* Spot flour with $0.5\%\ \mathrm{KI} + 2\mathrm{M}\ \mathrm{HCl} \rightarrow$ **black-purple spots** (liberated $\mathrm{I_2}$ + starch).
* **Alloxan:** Formed when maida is bleached with $\mathrm{ClO_2}$. Selectively enters pancreatic $\beta$-cells via **GLUT2** $\rightarrow$ generates hydroxyl radicals ($\mathrm{\cdot OH}$) $\rightarrow$ necrotic destruction of $\beta$-cells $\rightarrow$ **Type-1 Diabetes**.

## 3. Adulteration in Dairy Products

### 3.1 Synthetic Milk & Detection Summary
Fraudulent synthetic milk mimics natural milk using:
* **Vegetable Oil:** Provides emulsified fat ($3.5-4.5\%$).
* **Detergents / Surfactants (LAS):** Emulsifier to keep oil suspended; creates persistent foam.
* **Urea ($\mathrm{CO(NH_2)_2}$):** Inflates specific gravity and non-protein nitrogen (NPN).
* **Neutralizers ($\mathrm{NaOH, NaHCO_3}$):** Neutralizes lactic acid to prevent curdling on boiling.
* **Starch:** Restores thickness and Solids-Not-Fat (SNF).
* **Formalin ($37\%\ \mathrm{HCHO}$):** Biocide preservative preventing souring.

| Adulterant | Rapid Chemical Test | Positive Observation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Water** | Lactometer Reading / Cryoscopy | Lactometer $<28$; Freezing point closer to $0^\circ\text{C}$ (normal: $-0.540^\circ\text{C}$) |
| **Starch** | $1\%\ \mathrm{I_2 / KI}$ Iodine Solution | **Intense blue-black color** |
| **Urea** | DMAB Reagent ($p$-dimethylaminobenzaldehyde) | **Intense yellow-green color** |
| **Detergent** | Methylene Blue + Chloroform | **Lower chloroform layer turns blue** |
| **Neutralizers** | $0.1\%$ Rosalic Acid in ethanol | **Rose-red color** (genuine milk is brownish-orange) |
| **Formalin** | **Hehner's Test** (conc. $\mathrm{H_2SO_4} + \text{trace }\mathrm{FeCl_3}$) | **Violet / purple ring** at interface (tryptophan adduct) |

### 3.2 Melamine Triazine Fraud
* **Analytical Loophole:** Melamine ($\mathrm{C_3H_6N_6}$) contains **$66.6\%$ nitrogen**. Standard Kjeldahl tests calculate crude protein as $\% N \times 6.38$, blindly reading melamine as high-value protein.
* **Toxicity:** Melamine combines with cyanuric acid via hydrogen bonds to form insoluble **[Melamine $\cdot$ Cyanuric Acid]** crystals in renal tubules $\rightarrow$ **Kidney stones, acute renal failure**.

### 3.3 Ghee and Butter Adulteration
* **Vanaspati (Hydrogenated Oil) in Ghee:** Hydrogenation converts unsaturated oils to trans-fatty acids (**elaidic acid**). Drops Reichert-Meissl (RM) value from $26-32$ to $<1.0$.
* **Baudouin Test:** Vanaspati contains statutory $5\%$ sesame oil. Sesame oil has **sesamolin**, which hydrolyzes with $\mathrm{HCl}$ to **sesamol**. Sesamol condenses with added **furfural** $\rightarrow$ **crimson-rose red color** in acid layer.

## 4. Adulteration in Edible Oils and Fats

### 4.1 Argemone Oil (*Argemone mexicana*) in Mustard Oil
* **Seeds:** Mustard seeds are smooth and spherical; *Argemone* seeds are wrinkled, deeply pitted with a **pointed apical beak**.
* **Toxin:** Quaternary alkaloid **Sanguinarine**.
* **Pathology (Epidemic Dropsy):**
  1. Competitively inhibits membrane **$\mathrm{Na^+/K^+}$-ATPase** $\rightarrow$ intracellular $\mathrm{Na^+}$ retention and cellular edema.
  2. Hyper-activates **eNOS**, releasing massive **Nitric Oxide ($\mathrm{NO}$)** $\rightarrow$ widespread capillary hyperpermeability and fluid leakage into tissues.
  3. *Symptoms:* Severe bilateral pitting edema of legs, secondary open-angle glaucoma, cardiac failure.
* **Detection Tests:**
  * *Nitric Acid Test:* Oil + conc. $\mathrm{HNO_3} \rightarrow$ **crimson-red ring** at interface.
  * *$\mathrm{FeCl_3}$ Test:* Saponify oil, acidify, heat with $\mathrm{FeCl_3} \rightarrow$ reddish-brown needle crystals.
  * *TLC:* Sanguinarine gives bright **golden-yellow/orange fluorescence under UV ($366\text{ nm}$)**.

### 4.2 Other Oil Adulterants
* **Mineral Oil (Holde's Test):** Edible oils saponify with alcoholic $\mathrm{KOH}$ to water-soluble soaps. Non-saponifiable mineral oil separates upon adding boiling water, creating **persistent milky turbidity**.
* **Cottonseed Oil (Halphen Test):** Contains toxic **gossypol** and cyclopropenoid fatty acids (malvalic, sterculic). Heated with $1\%$ sulfur in $\mathrm{CS_2}$ $\rightarrow$ **deep rose-red color**.
* **Used Cooking Oil (UCO):** Repeated frying causes thermal oxidation and polymerization. **FSSAI safety limit: Total Polar Compounds (TPC) $\le 25\%$**.

## 6. High-Yield Exam Mnemonics

* **D-R-O-P-S-Y (Argemone / Sanguinarine):**
  * **D** -- Dilation of capillaries via $\mathrm{NO}$
  * **R** -- Redness, rash, and cutaneous erythema
  * **O** -- Oxidative phosphorylation uncoupled in mitochondria
  * **P** -- Pitting dependent lower limb edema
  * **S** -- Sanguinarine inhibits $\mathrm{Na^+/K^+}$-ATPase
  * **Y** -- Yellow UV fluorescence on TLC plate ($366\text{ nm}$)
* **F-U-N-D-S (Milk Rapid Tests):**
  * **F** -- Formalin: Hehner's violet ring
  * **U** -- Urea: DMAB yellow-green
  * **N** -- Neutralizers: Rosalic acid rose-red
  * **D** -- Detergent: Methylene blue chloroform layer
  * **S** -- Starch: Iodine deep blue
* **B-A-U-D-O-U-I-N (Vanaspati in Ghee):**
  * **B** -- By-product sesamol
  * **A** -- Acid ($\mathrm{HCl}$) hydrolysis
  * **U** -- Unsaponifiable marker
  * **D** -- Dialdehyde/Aldehyde: Furfural
  * **O** -- Overlay turns **Rose-Red**
  * **I** -- Indicates Vanaspati ($5\%$ sesame oil)
  * **N** -- Negative in pure ghee
* **O-D-A-P (Khesari Dhal Neurotoxin):**
  * **O** -- Oxalyl radical ($\beta$-ODAP / BOAA)
  * **D** -- Depolarization via AMPA receptors
  * **A** -- Arhar dhal fraudulently substituted
  * **P** -- Paraplegia (Neurolathyrism with scissoring gait)

## 7. High-Yield Solved Question Bank

### 7.1 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

1. **Which neurotoxin in Khesari Dhal causes neurolathyrism?**  
   *(a) Sanguinarine \quad (b) $\beta$-ODAP \quad (c) Coumarin \quad (d) Alloxan*  
   **Ans: (b)** $\beta$-ODAP ($\mathrm{BOAA}$) excites AMPA receptors, destroying motor neurons.

2. **Hehner's test detects formalin in milk by reacting with which amino acid in casein?**  
   *(a) Methionine \quad (b) Cysteine \quad (c) Tryptophan \quad (d) Phenylalanine*  
   **Ans: (c)** Formaldehyde condenses with tryptophan indole ring in presence of $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ to form a violet ring.

3. **Baudouin test detects Vanaspati in ghee via condensation of furfural with:**  
   *(a) Gossypol \quad (b) Sesamol \quad (c) Sanguinarine \quad (d) Ricinoleic acid*  
   **Ans: (b)** Sesamolin in sesame oil hydrolyzes to sesamol, which turns crimson-red with furfural.

4. **Which enzyme is inhibited by Sanguinarine in Epidemic Dropsy?**  
   *(a) Acetylcholinesterase \quad (b) $\mathrm{Na^+/K^+}$-ATPase \quad (c) Cytochrome c oxidase \quad (d) Amylase*  
   **Ans: (b)** Inhibits sodium-potassium pump, inducing cellular edema.

5. **Melamine is added to milk to deceive which test?**  
   *(a) RM value \quad (b) Brix \quad (c) Kjeldahl crude protein \quad (d) Iodine value*  
   **Ans: (c)** Melamine contains $66.6\%$ nitrogen, inflating apparent protein ($\% N \times 6.38$).

6. **Metanil yellow in pulses is confirmed when conc. $\mathrm{HCl}$ produces:**  
   *(a) Emerald green \quad (b) Persistent magenta-pink \quad (c) White turbidity \quad (d) Effervescence*  
   **Ans: (b)** Protonation forms a resonance-stabilized magenta-red quinoid dication.

7. **Holde's test detects mineral oil in vegetable oil by:**  
   *(a) Saponification turbidity \quad (b) Fluorescent color \quad (c) Acidity \quad (d) Iodine absorption*  
   **Ans: (a)** Saponified vegetable fats dissolve; non-saponifiable mineral oil creates persistent milky turbidity.

8. **Cassia bark is harmful because it contains high levels of:**  
   *(a) Eugenol \quad (b) Coumarin \quad (c) Cinnamaldehyde \quad (d) Curcumin*  
   **Ans: (b)** Coumarin ($0.2-1.0\%$) metabolizes to a 3,4-epoxide causing liver necrosis.

9. **FSSAI maximum permissible Total Polar Compounds (TPC) in frying oil is:**  
   *(a) $10\%$ \quad (b) $15\%$ \quad (c) $25\%$ \quad (d) $40\%$*  
   **Ans: (c)** Cooking oil exceeding $25\%$ TPC is legally adulterated and toxic.

10. **Papaya seeds are distinguished from black pepper because they:**  
    *(a) Sink in water \quad (b) Float on water/alcohol \quad (c) Turn blue with iodine \quad (d) Dissolve*  
    **Ans: (b)** Due to lower density and internal air spaces, papaya seeds float; black pepper sinks.

### 7.2 Short Answer Questions (SAQs)

**Q1. State the principle of the Baudouin Test.**  
*Ans:* Vanaspati contains $5\%$ sesame oil by regulation. Sesamolin in sesame oil is hydrolyzed by conc. $\mathrm{HCl}$ into sesamol (3,4-methylenedioxyphenol). Sesamol condenses with furfural in acid medium to give a distinct, persistent **crimson-rose red color**. Pure ghee gives no color.

**Q2. What is Epidemic Dropsy and its cause?**  
*Ans:* An acute toxic condition caused by consuming mustard oil adulterated with *Argemone mexicana* seed oil containing **Sanguinarine**. Sanguinarine inhibits $\mathrm{Na^+/K^+}$-ATPase and triggers massive Nitric Oxide release, causing bilateral lower-limb pitting edema, secondary open-angle glaucoma, and cardiac failure.

**Q3. Why does Kjeldahl method fail to detect Melamine in milk?**  
*Ans:* Kjeldahl digests all nitrogenous compounds into $(\mathrm{NH_4})_2\mathrm{SO_4}$ and calculates protein by multiplying total nitrogen by $6.38$. Because melamine has $66.6\%$ nitrogen, it deceptively inflates the calculated protein value without distinguishing peptide bonds from triazine rings.

**Q4. Distinguish true Ceylon Cinnamon from Cassia.**  
*Ans:* Ceylon cinnamon has multiple paper-thin, soft, delicate quills with $<0.004\%$ coumarin (safe). Cassia has a single, thick, woody hollow roll with high coumarin ($0.2-1.0\%$), which is hepatotoxic.

**Q5. How does Potassium Bromate affect bread dough?**  
*Ans:* $\mathrm{KBrO_3}$ oxidizes cysteine thiol groups ($-\mathrm{SH}$) in wheat gluten into covalent disulfide bonds ($-\mathrm{S-S-}$), creating an elastic matrix that traps $\mathrm{CO_2}$ and inflates loaf volume. It is a banned IARC 2B renal carcinogen.

### 7.3 Quantitative Worked Numericals

**Problem 1 (Melamine Correction):**  
*A milk powder sample has $0.7844\%$ total nitrogen. (a) Find apparent crude protein ($F = 6.38$). (b) If $0.15\%$ of powder weight is Melamine ($66.63\%\ \mathrm{N}$), calculate true dairy protein.*  
*Solution:*
1. $\text{Apparent Protein} = 0.7844\% \times 6.38 = \mathbf{5.004\%}$.
2. Nitrogen from melamine in $100\text{ g}$ sample $= 0.15\text{ g} \times 0.6663 = 0.0999\text{ g } \mathrm{N}$.  
   True dairy nitrogen $= 0.7844\% - 0.0999\% = 0.6845\%$.
3. $\text{True Protein} = 0.6845\% \times 6.38 = \mathbf{4.367\%}$.  
   *(Melamine falsely inflated protein by $+14.6\%$).*

**Problem 2 (RM Value Dilution):**  
*A suspected cow ghee sample has an RM value of $14.5$. If pure cow ghee RM is $28.0$ and Vanaspati RM is $0.6$, calculate the percentage of Vanaspati.*  
*Solution:*
$$\% \text{Vanaspati} = \frac{RM_{\text{pure}} - RM_{\text{sample}}}{RM_{\text{pure}} - RM_{\text{vanaspati}}} \times 100 = \frac{28.0 - 14.5}{28.0 - 0.6} \times 100 = \frac{13.5}{27.4} \times 100 = \mathbf{49.27\%}$$
