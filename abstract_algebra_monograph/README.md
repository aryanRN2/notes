# 🧮 Abstract Algebra Monograph & Advanced Lecture Suite

Comprehensive university monograph and lecture suites covering Groups, Rings, Fields, Actions, and Polynomial Domains.

## 📄 Documents & Monograph
- **`Abstract_Algebra_Monograph.pdf`** / **`main.tex`**: Complete 5-Unit Monograph with formal definitions, lemmas, theorems, and end-of-unit review problems.
- **`toc_gen/`**: Dedicated book cover and table of contents generation suite.

## 🏛️ Monograph Units
- **`unit1_groups_foundations.tex`**: Groups, subgroups, cyclic groups, Lagrange's theorem, and order calculations.
- **`unit2_quotients_isomorphisms_products.tex`**: Normal subgroups, quotient groups, First/Second/Third Isomorphism Theorems, and direct products.
- **`unit3_symmetric_alternating_groups.tex`**: Symmetric groups $S_n$, alternating groups $A_n$, cycle decompositions, and simplicity of $A_5$.
- **`unit4_actions_class_equation_rings.tex`**: Group actions, orbit-stabilizer theorem, conjugacy classes, Class Equation, and introduction to rings.
- **`unit5_domains_fractions_polynomials_algebras.tex`**: Integral domains, field of fractions, polynomial rings, irreducible elements, UFDs, and PIDs.

## 📝 Lecture Suites (`shreya_lecture_notes/`)
- **`abstract_algebra_complete_notes.pdf`** / **`.tex`**: Comprehensive lecture course transcripts with full step-by-step proofs.
- **`abstract_algebra_part2.pdf`** / **`.tex`**: Part II continuation focusing on ring homomorphisms, ideals, and quotient rings.
