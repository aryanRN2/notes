# 🌐 Metric Spaces Comprehensive Monograph

Comprehensive second-edition textbook monograph on Metric Spaces and Modern Analysis by Pawan K. Jain and Khalil Ahmad, typeset in high-fidelity LaTeX.

## 📄 Master Monograph
- **`Metric_Spaces_Comprehensive_Monograph.pdf`** / **`main.tex`**: Full publication-grade monograph with complete proofs, geometric explanations, and exercises.

## 📖 Chapter Breakdown
- **`ch01_preliminaries.tex`**: Real analysis foundations, inequalities (Hölder, Minkowski, Cauchy-Schwarz).
- **`ch02_introductory_concepts.tex`**: Metric definitions, Euclidean/Manhattan metrics, open/closed balls, limit points, interior, and boundary.
- **`ch03_completeness.tex`**: Cauchy sequences, Cantor Intersection Theorem, Baire Category Theorem, and completion of metric spaces.
- **`ch04_continuous_functions.tex`**: Continuity, uniform continuity, homeomorphisms, and isometry.
- **`ch05_compactness.tex`**: Compact metric spaces, Heine-Borel theorem, Bolzano-Weierstrass property, and total boundedness.
- **`ch06_connectedness.tex`**: Connected and path-connected spaces, components, and preservation under continuous maps.
- **`ch07_fixed_point_theorems.tex`**: Banach Contraction Principle and applications to differential equations.
- **`appendices.tex`**: Supplementary reference tables and symbol indices.
