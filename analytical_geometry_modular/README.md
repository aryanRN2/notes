# 📐 Analytical Geometry: Modular Lecture Suite

Page-by-page manuscript transcriptions enhanced with TikZ vector graphics, detailed coordinate derivations, and examination problems by [sciqb](https://sciqb.com).

## 📄 Master Notes
- **`analytical_geometry_notes.pdf`** / **`analytical_geometry_notes.tex`**: Complete enhanced lecture notes (Pages 1–69).

## 📐 Chapters & Modules
- **`ch1_polar_conics.tex`**: Polar coordinate foundations, focus-directrix forms, tangents, normals, and polar chords.
- **`ch2_coordinates_dc_dr.tex`**: 3D rectangular coordinates, direction cosines, and direction ratios.
- **`ch3_the_plane.tex`**: General equation of the plane, intercept form, normal form, and angle between planes.
- **`ch4_straight_line_skew.tex`**: Symmetrical form of lines, skew lines, and shortest distance.
- **`ch5_the_sphere.tex`**: Sphere equation, plane sections, tangent planes, and orthogonal spheres.
- **`ch5_vectors.tex`**: Vector triple products and reciprocal system of vectors.
- **`ch6_the_sphere.tex`**: Radical planes and coaxial systems of spheres.
- **`appendix_cheat_sheet.tex`**: Formulas, standard equations, and quick review cheat sheet.
